Hari Browser is a privacy-focused Android browser built on Android System WebView.

FEATURES
• HTTPS-only navigation and cleartext traffic blocked by default.
• Android WebView Safe Browsing and strict mixed-content handling.
• Best-effort local ad and tracker host blocking.
• Third-party cookies disabled by default.
• Separate private-browsing process/profile with best-effort cleanup.
• Encrypted bookmarks and optional encrypted browsing history using Android Keystore.
• Tabs, downloads, page sharing, in-page find, reader-style view and desktop mode.
• QR-code generation and Android print-to-PDF support.
• Light and dark themes, five accent colours and offline home-screen wallpapers.
• Home-screen interface translations for English, Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Spanish, French and Arabic.
• Refreshed responsive home layout, improved localized quick actions, faster directory filtering and persistent home-page search-engine choice.
• Local tools, personal shortcuts and curated website directory.
• Optional Hari AI chat with API-key setup in Settings. Your key is encrypted on this device; prompts are sent online only when you choose to chat.
• No account requirement, app analytics, advertising SDKs or remote blocklist downloads.

PRIVACY NOTES
Hari Browser is not a guarantee of anonymity. Websites and your chosen search provider receive the requests needed to serve content. Safe Browsing and WebView are supplied by Android on your device. The built-in tracker blocker is best-effort and cannot block every tracker. Private mode cannot erase downloaded files, screenshots, network/provider logs or every artifact outside the app's control. The browser rejects HTTP pages rather than silently downgrading to an insecure connection, so some websites may not open.
