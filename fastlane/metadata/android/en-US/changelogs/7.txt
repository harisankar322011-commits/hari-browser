Hari Browser 2.3 adds optional Teen Protection with a 6–8 digit parent PIN, blocks a starter list of explicit adult and selected real-money gambling searches plus known adult/gambling domains, and applies strict SafeSearch for supported search providers. Hari AI now has a manual connection test, fallback when a model is unavailable, and clearer setup/quota errors. These are app-level safeguards, not complete parental control.
