Hari Browser 2.4.0
- Adds the HARI SINGULARITY five-layer protection dashboard and clearer disclosures for optional URL reputation checks.
- Improves download scan reporting and custom local tracker-list support.
- Bounds session-only site approvals and restores the previously open HTTPS page after a WebView renderer crash.
- Local file checks and site-safety signals are best-effort; they do not prove that a website or file is safe.

Important: the previously reported Android device-protection warning must be investigated against a freshly built and independently scanned APK. Do not ignore a device warning.
- Safely opens validated email and dialer links only after confirmation; unsupported schemes remain blocked.
- Hari AI now displays the exact model ID used for each successful reply.
- Caps optional URLhaus reputation work at four concurrent lookups and 128 cached definitive results.
- Removes an obsolete synchronous encrypted-store read path from the browser activity.
